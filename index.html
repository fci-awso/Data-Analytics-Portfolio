What every project shares

Production-grade analytics — no backend

Every dashboard runs entirely in the browser. Upload a CSV and get instant KPIs, interactive charts, and business insights.

100% Client-Side

No server, no build step, no npm. Just open index.html — everything runs in the browser.

Dynamic CSV Upload

Drag & drop any compatible CSV. The dashboard recalculates every KPI, chart, and insight instantly.

Full EN / AR + RTL

Complete bilingual experience with proper right-to-left layout — including chart labels and legends.

Multi-Theme System

Four to five themes per dashboard, persisted in localStorage, adapting charts and UI in real time.

Insight Engine

Deterministic analytics generate prioritized business insights with recommendations — not fabricated ML.

Mobile-First

Every dashboard has been hand-tuned for mobile — drawers, offcanvas filters, and responsive charts.
